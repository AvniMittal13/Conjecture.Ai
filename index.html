Your personal PhD assistant.

From a rough idea to a complete research paper. Conjecture.AI handles the hypothesis, the experimentation, and the writing — so you can focus on thinking.

Give it a direction like "mechanistic interpretability for multilingual models." It surveys the literature, forms hypotheses, runs experiments on your compute, critiques its own results, iterates, and produces a publication-ready draft.

Available end of May 2026 as a VS Code extension.

Three phases. One pipeline.

  • 01 Hypothesize.Surveys literature, identifies gaps, and generates testable hypotheses grounded in your available compute.
  • 02 Experiment.Pilots, full runs, ablations, figures. Critiques its own results and decides what to run next.
  • 03 Write.A complete paper — abstract through conclusion — that you can submit with minimal tweaks.

rough idea → literature review → hypothesis → experiments → self-critique → paper
